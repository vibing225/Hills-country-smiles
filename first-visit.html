Your First Visit

Start with a free, comfortable first visit.

We look forward to meeting you, showing you around our office and helping you understand your orthodontic options.

What to expect

Your initial exam is always free.

Your initial visit with us is free. The screening examination is used to determine the need for orthodontic treatment. Dr. April Brown will discuss her findings and any concerns you may have.

Sometimes a patient may not be ready for orthodontic treatment. These patients are placed on a growth observation recall, and we follow up annually until treatment is required or necessary. There is no charge for these visits.

  • Determine the optimum time to start treatment
  • Minimize time in braces
  • Work toward superior results

If treatment is recommended

Clear next steps, without the guesswork.

Complete diagnostic records are necessary before treatment begins. A separate fee is charged for these records.

Photographic images

Photos help document your teeth and treatment needs as part of the diagnostic record.

Orthodontic X-rays

X-rays provide information needed to understand the underlying dental structures.

Models of the teeth

These records give the doctor a complete picture to finalize your treatment plan.
