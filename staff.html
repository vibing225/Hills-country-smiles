Orthodontist

Dr. April Brown

Dr. Brown was born and raised in Hope, Arkansas. She graduated from Hope High School as valedictorian, earned her Bachelor of Science degree Summa Cum Laude from Ouachita Baptist University, and completed her Doctorate of Dental Medicine and Masters in Oral Biology at the University of Louisville.

In 2008, she completed a three-year orthodontic residency at the University of Texas at San Antonio Health Science Center. During residency, she treated patients ages 7 to 71 with a variety of orthodontic needs.

Dr. Brown is married to her childhood sweetheart, Jesse Brown. They have a son, Jack, and a daughter, Ava. She enjoys hunting, fishing, cooking and singing.
